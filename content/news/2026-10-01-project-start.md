---
title: STAMP project starts
summary: The STAMP project officially started on 1 October 2026.
---

The STAMP project, *Stochastic integrated task and motion planning for human-robot
collaboration*, officially started on 1 October 2026 at LAMOR, University of Zagreb Faculty of
Electrical Engineering and Computing. It is funded by the Croatian Science Foundation.

Over the next three years we will develop methods that let robots collaborate with people
safely and efficiently, even when the future is uncertain.
