## STAMP HRC dataset

A dataset of human motion and contextual information recorded in collaborative scenarios. It
includes OptiTrack motion capture, RGB-D streams, task annotations and environment maps. The
participant pool will be gender-balanced and diverse across age, stature and handedness.
